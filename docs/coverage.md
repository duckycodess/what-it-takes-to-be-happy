## The assignment's four parts

| Part | Where it is answered | The answer, in short |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What do you think of happiness? | `s202` `s230` | Koj emphasizes living well; TJ defends ordinary enjoyment and a life worth returning to; Vier emphasizes agency. Their endorsed positions overlap without becoming identical. |
| What constitutes happiness? | `s204` `s206` `s229` | Enjoyment, fulfilment, rest, relationships and agency matter together; security, time and access affect which possibilities can be sustained. This is an argument, not a universal checklist. |
| Is it achievable in the society we have today? | `s224` | Yes, but unequally sustained. Joy under difficult conditions is real without proving that deprivation is harmless or that everyone has equal room to choose. |
| If not, how could it be achieved? | `s225` `s226` `s227` `s228` | Individual, community and institutional changes must reduce barriers without blaming people or transferring burdens unseen; proposed improvements need evaluation and must be allowed to fail. |

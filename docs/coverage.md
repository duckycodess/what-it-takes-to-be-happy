## The assignment's four parts

The midterm question has four parts ([instruction snapshot](notes/2026-10-08-instructions.md)).
Each is answered explicitly on the main path, not implied.

| Part | Where it is answered | The answer, in short |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What do you think of happiness? | `s005`–`s015` | Three definitions that overlap and do not merge: living well (Koj), room to live (TJ), a relationship with life you can participate in (Vier). `s014` holds them side by side; `s015` admits each one is itself a standard. |
| What constitutes happiness? | `s006`, `s011`, `s013`, `s016`–`s029`, `s045`–`s056` | Pleasure, meaning, growth, and rest negotiated rather than ranked (`s029`); relationships and ordinary repeated care (`s045`–`s056`); and, running under all of it, conditions — time, money, access (`s057`–`s075`). |
| Is it achievable in the society we have today? | `s075`, restated at `s076` | Yes, and unequally. Small joys are real for people with very little; the chance to sustain them is not evenly distributed. |
| If not, how could it be achieved? | `s077`–`s082`, bounded by `s081` | Personal practices offered without blame, campus and community changes, institutional direction — with who-benefits-who-pays applied to our own proposals, and an explicit statement that we evaluated no specific policy. |

## Reading measurement

Measured with `./tools/count-main-path.sh`. Re-run it after any change to the prose.

| Measure | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Main-path words | 8,548 at the time of writing; re-run the tool after any prose change |
| Scenes | 87 |
| Mean words per scene | 98 |
| Words-per-minute assumption | 200–250, silent reading of ordinary nonfiction |
| Reading estimate | roughly 35–43 minutes |

**What counts.** Narrator prose, dialogue, and visible caveats — everything inside a blockquote in a
scene block. That is exactly what a reader sees in presentation view, in the full reading view, and
in print.

**What does not count.** Editorial metadata (beat purpose, cast, visual purpose, static description,
transition, provenance, status), chapter headers and the chapter index, the handoff section, source
citations in panels, and optional detail — which is supplementary by definition.

**This is longer than the 15–30 minutes the brief anticipated, and we did not cut to fit.**
`docs/brief.md` sets that range as an approximate commitment "to be estimated from actual content",
and states explicitly that it "is not a hard cap that deletes distinct perspectives". Getting to 30
minutes would mean removing about a quarter of the main path. The longest scenes are the ones
carrying quoted evidence and its stated limits (`s069`, `s053`, `s064`, `s038`, `s087`), and the
acceptance criteria require those limits to stay on the main path rather than hide in a panel.

If a shorter main path is wanted, these are the levers, in the order we would use them:

1. Move the longer source quotations in `s020`, `s050`, `s062` and `s069` into their source panels,
   keeping one sentence of each on the main path. Roughly 300 words.
2. Compress `s041`, `s043` and `s066` into two scenes. Roughly 200 words.
3. Fold `s031` into `s030` and `s048` into `s047`. Roughly 250 words.

We would not go further than that without losing a perspective, a caveat, or a piece of evidence.

## Delta reconciliation

New contributions arrive as files under `docs/notes/deltas/`, following
[the delta protocol](notes/README.md) and [the template](notes/delta-template.md).

**Inventory as of 9 October 2026: no deltas exist.** `docs/notes/deltas/` has not been created, and
the three Google Doc tab snapshots captured on 8 October 2026 remain the complete contributed
material. Nothing in this storyboard is pending a reconciliation.

When a delta does arrive, reconcile it like this:

1. Read it against this matrix. Find the rows it changes — a member position, a lens, a source, or
   an assignment part.
2. If it adds a source, give it the next unused identifier in [the research register](research.md)
   and write its evidence record before any scene cites it. Never repoint an existing identifier at
   a different work.
3. If it changes a member's position, update the member table above and the scenes listed in that
   row. A position may be revised; it may not be invented, and it may not be moved to another
   member.
4. If it needs a new scene, take the next unused scene number and insert it into the relevant
   chapter's order list. Do not renumber anything.
5. Re-run `./tools/check-storyboard.sh` and `./tools/count-main-path.sh`, and update the measured
   figures above.
6. Keep the disagreement. If the delta contradicts something already in the storyboard, revise the
   claim and its limitation in place rather than appending a contradicting paragraph at the end.

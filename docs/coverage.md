# Coverage matrix

What the essay covers, where, and what it measures. Everything points at stable scene identifiers
in [the approved scene script](redesign/approved-scene-script.md), which is the source the website
is transferred from, and at [the published document](../content/content.json).

This matrix is for the 45-scene rewrite (`s088`–`s132`). It replaces the 87-beat matrix of the
earlier build; that argument and its scene identifiers are historical and are recorded in
[docs/storyboard.md](storyboard.md), not here. The audit of how far each source was read is
[docs/research.md](research.md) and is not repeated below.

## The assignment's four parts

The approved script names where each part of the captured prompt is answered. Each is answered
explicitly on the main reading path; none is implied or left to a supplementary view.

| Part | Where it is answered | The answer, in short |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What do you think of happiness? | `s089`, set up by `s088` and tested through `s091` | Three answers that overlap without merging: living well with purpose, relationships, choices and room to grow (Koj); a life worth returning to, with people, time, security and ordinary enjoyment (TJ); a relationship with life in which you can still take part and make choices (Vier). |
| What constitutes happiness? | `s129`, built through `s092`–`s121` | Pleasure, fulfilment, rest, relationships and agency, with time, security and access shaping which of them a person can pursue and sustain. Explicitly not a checklist and not a single score. |
| Is it achievable in the society we have today? | `s122` | Yes, and unequally. People can enjoy things, value their lives and exercise agency under difficult conditions; the opportunity to sustain that is not evenly distributed. |
| If not, how could it be achieved? | `s123`–`s127` | Personal practices offered without blame (`s123`), campus and community changes (`s124`), conditions institutions can change (`s125`), who-benefits-who-pays applied to our own proposals (`s126`), and a test that allows the proposal to fail (`s127`). |

## What this matrix does not claim

- It records where the argument is made, not whether a reader agrees with it.
- The audit levels are the ones recorded on 9 October 2026. They describe what was read then; they
  are not a claim that every URL was re-opened for this rewrite.
- The dialogue is reconstructed from the group's own notes, and the approved script records the
  basis for each line in its dialogue-provenance table. It is not a transcript, and the pictured
  situations are illustrations rather than reported events.
- `./tools/count-main-path.sh` measures the main path; the figure is editorial and is not shown to
  a reader.

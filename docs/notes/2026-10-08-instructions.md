MIDTERM REQUIREMENT INSTRUCTIONS

Choose ONLY ONE out of the five questions to answer. When you’ve chosen a question, discuss with your group how you would like to present your reflection and answer. Possible submissions are as follows:

- Video (at least 6 minutes)

- Audio recording (at least 6 minutes)

- Essay (at least 1,000 words)

- Zine (at least 8 pages)

- Any format you desire that I may not have considered (but with equivalent depth and content with the above-mentioned possible submissions)

The questions:

- What do you think of happiness? What do you think constitutes happiness? Is your definition of happiness achievable in the kind of society we have today? If not, how can it be achieved (i.e. changed systems? New innovations? Etc)?

How to answer these questions:

The essence of the questions considers the following: partially personal ideas and partially research. I highly encourage that you write and express your ideas. I also encourage you to include personal observations and experiences. However, please contextualize what you say. Interrogate possible assumptions you may have. I also implore you to back up your assertions with data and published research. I allow students to also explore non-academic readings/ideas (substack, wordpress, tweet, reddit, etc) but please be discerning about what you reference. Furthermore, these “gray literature” should not be your only source of information.

I know there is an assumption that you will need to answer correctly. However, rather than a “correct” answer, what I seek is a nuanced answer. This means that I would like you to explore as much viewpoints as possible and reflect this exploration in your submission. Feel free to explore as much as you want and be guided by your curiosity. I have capacity to hold multiple viewpoints and as such I will not base my marking on a singular correct answer. But just an emphasis on this reminder: interrogate your assumptions and biases (examples of biases: thinking that a discipline – science/social science/etc -- is the only correct viewpoint; thinking that science/social science/etc as discipline is devoid of politics; thinking that academia is the only valid expertise; etc). From which position or baseline are you answering these questions from?

You don’t need to provide a reference list based on any referencing guide. But please, mention your sources or where you found your information.

How I will mark your work:

You will be graded based on the following criteria:

- Depth and thoroughness of discussion

- Clear and logical argumentation

- Reflection (inward curiosity about how you made a conclusion)

- Use of concepts, ideas, and information discussed in class

How to submit:

Please save as a digital file in the submission folder (midterm) in the class Gdrive. Write/indicate your names in the file (as part of the document or as file name). Peer grading is not necessary; but if you wish to talk to me about your groupmate’s contribution, feel free to let me know (and hopefully be open to mediation if you consent). Deadline is on 9 October 2026 at 11:59 PM

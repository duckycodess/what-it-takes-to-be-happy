Status: implementation plan and owner amendment, **not** approval of replacement prose or production promotion. The approved 45-scene script and its generated content remain the only reader copy until an exact-copy Markdown replacement is separately approved. No word, part or length cap is imposed. The October 9 hosted-site amendment still governs delivery; #7 owns release-output cleanup.

## Reader structure

1. Preserve landing and the existing scene addresses, essay order, member voices, sources, comparisons and artwork. On a wide viewport, build a persistent left chapter rail next to a broader essay canvas. Each chapter title is always visible; only the current chapter's parts start expanded. Other chapters can be expanded individually without closing the current one. Put the whole-rail collapse control in the rail, with an obvious restore control that remains visible outside it; re-anchor on the current scene after width reflow, rather than promising an unchanged pixel scroll offset. Reuse approved `Chapters` for the rail toggle and restore, the approved scene titles as part labels, and `aria-expanded` for state. If genuinely new reader-facing strings prove necessary, stop and seek owner approval of the script/transfer before integration; never invent them on this branch.
2. At phone width, hide the persistent rail but offer a compact `Chapters` trigger and dismissible drawer containing the entire navigation, including continuous reading and Sources & Credits. Opening moves focus inside, Escape and the close control restore it to the trigger, navigating to a part closes the drawer before focusing the destination, and hidden drawer content cannot be tabbed. Reuse `Chapters` for close with `aria-expanded` state. Avoid a focus trap unless the drawer actually behaves as a modal; if modal, guard background focus. Indicate the current part through `aria-current` on its link, not an extra live region; update it and expanded-chapter state on navigation/history and continuous scrolling. Link each part by its stable scene ID; chapter title links retain first-scene navigation. Preserve the existing #chapters landmark and #essay skip target. Selecting a part in continuous mode scrolls within that mode.
3. Move the approved `Read continuously` and `Sources & Credits` links into a separate secondary section of the shared desktop rail/phone drawer; preserve source query/fragment return semantics and landing's one source link, and keep the new Sources link in `writeSourcesLinks`. Bottom fixed reading footer holds only Back and Next; Back on first returns to landing, Next on last is absent and Read again may appear with the final passage, not in the footer. No visible Print or progress/counter controls. Browser print retains all chapters, every block and both comparison states.
4. Keep programmatic scene focus/announcement after navigation without a forced `.scene:focus` outline; retain `.scene:focus-visible` for keyboard-initiated focus, focus-visible interactive controls and meaningful source destination focus. Verify pointer and arrow-key cases in Chrome. Avoid stealing focus on initial deep links. In continuous mode, the origin of a shared Sources link should follow the visible part, including keyboard activation.

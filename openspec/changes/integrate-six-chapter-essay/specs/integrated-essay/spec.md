# Spec Delta

## Purpose

The full essay is a single independently readable, evidence-qualified argument available in every reading mode of the hosted reader.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Complete ordered main path
The reader SHALL contain the authored opening and six shared questions in the storyboard's stable scene order, with all essential narrator paragraphs, member dialogue, visible caveats, transitions and ending present without opening optional surfaces. Each scene's ordered main-path references SHALL include each narration, dialogue and visible caveat exactly once; the landing SHALL contain s001's question/names/time without repeating them in the first beat.

#### Scenario: Read without optional detail
- **WHEN** a visitor reads from Begin through the last beat without expanding any detail
- **THEN** they encounter distinct TJ, Koj and Vier positions, the four assignment parts, evidence and limitations, personal/community/institutional changes, tradeoffs and unresolved questions without a universal happiness formula

#### Scenario: Follow stable identifiers
- **WHEN** a visitor revisits a chapter or follows a beat fragment after content reordering
- **THEN** the intended beat is addressed by its stable identifier and the ordered path follows the chapter scene lists rather than numeric array position

### Requirement: Honest attribution and standing
The reader SHALL distinguish shared research from member-endorsed positions and fictional illustration. A member line MUST trace to their note or later delta; every cited source SHALL retain its original identifier, evidence level and limitation. Inaccessible or abstract-only works MUST NOT be described as passage-level evidence.

#### Scenario: Inspect a source-backed position
- **WHEN** a reader views a sourced beat and its source entry
- **THEN** they see the claim and its relevant qualification on the beat, the source's own attribution and checked level, without needing to follow an external URL to understand its role

#### Scenario: Inspect a fictional example
- **WHEN** an invented friend appears or speaks
- **THEN** the presentation identifies the person as an illustration rather than a member's biography or population evidence

### Requirement: Equivalent complete reading routes
Presentation, continuous reading, scriptless and print routes SHALL carry the same essential argument and source context, including an honest reading-time estimate measured from the actual landing, chapter connections and main prose, and SHALL NOT expose fixture prose or a non-final badge.

#### Scenario: Read or print without interactions
- **WHEN** a visitor switches view, disables scripting, or prints at an arbitrary beat
- **THEN** the entire essay and source register remain readable in order and no essential claim exists only in an optional interaction

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Claim links lead to full, honestly scoped source records
The reader SHALL place numbered links [1]–[26] beside the corresponding approved claims and provide a separate Sources & Credits page with the approved introduction, class note, credits, method note, reference entries and evidence-access limits. R identities and original URLs SHALL not change.

#### Scenario: Source with abstract-only or publisher-description access
- **WHEN** the reader follows a claim referencing that source
- **THEN** the entry describes its actual access level, without presenting it as passage-level evidence.

### Requirement: Return retains the reader's context
The source page SHALL provide `Back to reading` that restores the originating scene and scene/continuous reading mode.

#### Scenario: First, middle or final scene
- **WHEN** a reader follows a numbered citation from any such scene in either reading mode and returns
- **THEN** the original scene and mode are restored without requiring a new search or advancing the story.
